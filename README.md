I'm an HR and talent strategist with 10+ years in enterprise technology, including Thomson Reuters, VMware, MongoDB, KPMG, and SAIC. I work on how people teams adopt AI: which work should change, who owns each decision, and how you know it helped. The projects below show that thinking in working form.

## Selected work

- **[People Leadership OS](https://github.com/ellehelvig/people-leadership-os):** how a Chief of Staff could run the Office of the CPO on GitHub. Issues are the intake, decision records are the memory, and Copilot drafts a weekly brief that a person edits. Start with the [weekly brief](https://github.com/ellehelvig/people-leadership-os/blob/main/briefs/2026-w46-cpo-brief.md).
- **[HR AI Transformation Playbook](https://github.com/ellehelvig/hr-ai-transformation-playbook):** a path from an AI idea to a governed pilot decision, covering use-case selection, work redesign, governance, enablement, and evaluation, with runnable synthetic examples. [Evidence and limits](https://github.com/ellehelvig/hr-ai-transformation-playbook/blob/main/docs/evidence-index.md).
- **[Resolve](https://github.com/ellehelvig/peopleops-resolution-agent):** a fictional HR request workflow that drafts answers from policy and pauses for human review. My tests showed keyword routing broke on paraphrased requests, so I published the failures and kept it on a production hold. [Try the demo](https://ellehelvig.github.io/peopleops-resolution-agent/) · [Case study](https://github.com/ellehelvig/peopleops-resolution-agent/blob/main/docs/case-study.md).
- **[Work Experiment Lab](https://elle-helvig-work-experiment-lab.lovable.app):** an interactive demo for choosing what to test, comparing a fictional three-arm pilot, and deciding what changes when speed improves but accuracy drops.
